ABOUT ALL1DOCPDF

Useful document tools.
Without the fuss.

All1DocPDF is a small, practical toolkit designed to make everyday PDF tasks simpler. Our goal is to help people manage documents quickly, without needing an account or installing a desktop application.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

What we do

All1DocPDF brings common document tasks together in one straightforward website. Depending on the tool, you can combine PDFs, extract pages, rotate pages, inspect basic PDF information, compress a PDF, or turn images into a PDF.

⌁

Simple by design

Clear tools and practical instructions, built to work on desktop and mobile browsers.

♧

Local processing

Supported file operations run in your browser; files are not intentionally uploaded to a All1DocPDF server.

✦

Free access

The current tools are offered free to use. Features and availability may change as the site develops.

How the tools work

All1DocPDF uses browser-based JavaScript libraries to process documents on your device. An internet connection is currently required to load some libraries and fonts from third-party content delivery networks. Processing performance depends on your device, browser, and document size.

Honest limitations

Compression may reduce visual quality and can turn text into images, which means the resulting PDF may no longer have selectable or searchable text. Compression does not guarantee a smaller output. This starter version does not include reliable PDF-to-Word conversion, OCR, or every advanced feature offered by desktop software. Always review important output files before relying on them.
